## Project in one paragraph

8-player Limit Hold'em research simulation studying whether observation-based
trust/reputation systems inherently reward exploitation. **Five** agent
architectures have now been tested: frozen rule-based (Phase 1), bounded
online optimization (Phase 2), **unbounded online optimization (Phase 2*,
NEW this session)**, LLM personality role-players (Phase 3), and LLM agents
with reasoning scaffolding (Phase 3.1 = CoT + per-opponent memory +
adaptive strategy notes). Headline result: a four-tier ladder of
trust-profit Pearson r values that takes the trap from r = -0.752 (frozen
rules) to r = -0.094 (LLMs with reasoning) — statistically
indistinguishable from broken at the population level. The new Phase 2*
sub-experiment **falsifies the "if agents have full freedom and economic
motivation, they converge to Nash" hypothesis** posed by mentor Arpit at
the 2026-04-30 meeting. Author: Rachit Agrawal (Polygence research
project, 2025-2026). Repo: https://github.com/RachitAgrawal146/Poker_trust .

* **Headline:** r = -0.609 +/- 0.221 (roughly equal to bounded P2's -0.637)
* **Mean parameter drift: 3.4 L1** (11x more than weak HC) -- agents moved
* **Cluster spread GREW** from 5.82 to 7.5+, mean convergence index 1.324
* **Falsification verdict:** Arpit's "they converge to Nash" hypothesis is
  decisively false. Agents diverge under stronger optimization, not
  converge.
* Three structural reasons:
  1. Trust posterior is non-stationary inside the optimization loop
     (lambda=0.95 decay means opponents react to stale beliefs about
     the agent ~70 hands after the agent changes strategy).
  2. 8 agents climbing simultaneously -> moving-target gradient.
  3. Axis-aligned coordinate descent in 36-dim is fundamentally slow even
     at larger step sizes.
* Per-seed r: -0.354, -0.700, -0.344, -0.887, -0.759 (variance triples
  vs bounded P2)
* Economic ordering preserved: Firestorm 6,512 chips (6x next archetype);

## Cross-phase story-hand narrative (entirely new)

The Polygence paper now has a curated "evolution story" arc told through
~10 specific hands across all 5 phases. Each hand was selected by a
phase-agnostic SQL fingerprint (`analysis/extract_story_hands.py`), then
the cross-phase mapping was written into
`paper_resources/interesting_hands/EVOLUTION_STORY.md`.

**The two money-quote hands** (most important narrative artifacts):

* **P3 hand #67** ("the trap"): LLM Wall calls a 4-bet preflop pot with
  **2♠ 5♣** (rank 6749, worst hand at the table). Calls flop, turn, river.
  Loses 35 chips to Firestorm's J-J. The LLM mechanically follows its
  "calling station" personality with no situational awareness.

* **P3.1 hand #146** ("the inversion"): Wall has K♥ Q♥ (rank 872). Firestorm
  raises preflop, c-bets flop, c-bets turn. Wall calls each street.
  Firestorm CHECKS the river -- a tell. Wall BETS the river. Firestorm
  calls with 8c Qc (rank 6075). **Wall wins 32 chips from Firestorm.**

47 total actions across two phases tells the entire project arc. Use these
as opening/closing examples in any talk.

**Notable finding from the extractor:** P3.1 returns NO hands matching
the "Wall pays off Firestorm" SQL fingerprint (slot A1.2). The trap-
microcosm dynamic has dissolved at the level of "is there even an example
to point at?".
